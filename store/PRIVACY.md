# MuK AI Browser: Privacy Policy

MuK AI Browser is a browser extension by MuK IT GmbH that connects the MuK AI
assistant of your own Odoo database to your browser.

## What the extension processes

The extension only talks to the Odoo address you enter in its options. It
does not send any data to MuK IT or to anyone else, and it contains no
analytics or tracking.

When you use it, the extension sends to your Odoo:

- the device key that pairs this browser with your Odoo user, created when you
  click Connect;
- the address and title of the tab the assistant works in;
- when the assistant acts, the content it asks for: the text and outline of
  the page, the results of clicks and form entries, and screenshots of that
  tab;
- a label for the device, such as "Chrome on Windows".

The extension reads a page only when the assistant asks for it, and only in
the tab the assistant works in. It never types into password, payment-card or
one-time-code fields and never uploads files.

## Where the data goes

All of the above goes to your Odoo database only, which you or your company
run or rent. What happens there, including which AI model provider Odoo sends
the conversation to, is set up in that Odoo and governed by its operator's
privacy policy.

## What is stored in the browser

The extension stores the Odoo address, the device key, the id of the open
chat and your preferences in Chrome's extension storage. Disconnect the
browser in the extension's options, or in Odoo under MuK AI > Browser >
Connected Browsers, to remove the key; removing the extension deletes all of
it.
